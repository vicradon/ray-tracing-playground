How to construct
The solution is hidden. Draw your own rays with the Bold line (L) and Construction line (C) tools, place your answer with Mark (X) when finding an image, then Evaluate (E). Construction lines are dotted and infinite, bold lines are solid segments; both snap to F, O, S, C, intersections (also with the focal planes), the lens or mirror itself, the point where the given ray hits the element, and the directions parallel to the axis, parallel to the given ray and through a point. On a mirror, a line started at the vertex S also snaps to the mirror image (about the axis) of a line ending there; hold Shift to turn this off briefly. On a touch screen, press, slide to the snap point and release to place each point.

How rays work
Every ray is traced through the elements in order: lens p′ = p − h/f, mirror the same followed by a reversal of direction. Aim a ray at any F, F′, O, S or C of any element, parallel to the axis, or at a free point (drag its handle). Images are found by intersecting the traced output rays of an object (dashed = virtual). Trace ray tool: drag from the object toward the element and release. The ray snaps to O, S, F, F′, C of any element, to parallel-to-axis, to the element at a grid height, or to a grid point. Undo/redo: buttons or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Right-click a ray and choose Trace parallel ray to add a ray with the same direction through a point you click. Right-click the canvas to add things or to remove/edit what is under the cursor (elements, objects, rays). Elements are traced from the leftmost; an object to the right of an element acts as a virtual object for it. On a touch screen, long-press instead of right-clicking, drag empty space with the Move tool to pan, and pinch to zoom.
